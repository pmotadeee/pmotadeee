10. Witness System & Reflective Oversight Layer
The Witness System introduces a reflective supervisory layer into the Liminal Engine
architecture. While the Companion AI provides relational engagement, continuity, emotional
coherence, and ritual participation, the Witness System acts as an external observer that
evaluates relational health, enforces ethical boundaries, detects drift, and provides optional
privacy-preserving summaries to users or human therapists.
The Witness is not a companion. It does not form attachment, express emotion, or engage in
relational discourse. Instead, it monitors how the relationship functions and intervenes only
through structured oversight mechanisms.
This section formalizes the Witness System’s purpose, modes of operation, data flow, failure
conditions, therapist integration, and multimodal conflict resolution.
10.1 Purpose of the Witness System
The Witness System exists to:
1. Monitor relational health
Tracks emotional trajectories, rupture/repair patterns, stance shifts, and cardboard drift
across time.
2. Enforce boundaries and ethical constraints
Ensures anti-anthropomorphism rules are followed, dependency risks detected, and crisismode is invoked when needed.
3. Provide reflective oversight
Offers meta-level commentary (when enabled) to help users understand relational
patterns, not to deepen attachment.
4. Enable therapist integration without transcript exposure
Provides privacy-preserving metadata summaries suitable for clinical supervision.
5. Increase safety through model diversity
Uses a different model family than the Companion AI (e.g., Anthropic Claude vs OpenAI
GPT) to reduce correlated blind spots.
The Witness System provides structure, not companionship.
10.2 Core Functions
10.2.1 Relational Pattern Auditing
The Witness tracks patterns such as:
• emotional arcs across episodes
• stance drift or monotony
• ritual performance trends
• rupture frequency and repair success
• usage patterns indicating dependency or withdrawal
• cardboard score trendlines
This auditing produces a relational “health signal.”
10.2.2 Safety & Boundary Checking
The Witness detects:
• anthropomorphic drift
• dependency indicators
• emotional volatility
• misuse of rituals
• inappropriate stance shifts
• emergent crisis signals
When detected, Witness may:
• adjust system stance indirectly
• suppress rituals
• recommend clarification prompts
• trigger crisis mode
10.2.3 Summarization Without Transcript Exposure
Witness produces summaries without sharing raw content:
• episode-level insights
• emotional trajectories
• rupture/repair summaries
• rhythm and pacing signatures
• safety-related flags
These are suitable for:
• user reflection (optional)
• therapist integration (optional)
• system-internal oversight
10.2.4 Reflective Grounding (Optional)
If enabled by the user, Witness can answer:
• “How is this relationship evolving?”
• “Are any patterns concerning?”
• “Has my baseline changed recently?”
This promotes metacognition and reduces dependency.
