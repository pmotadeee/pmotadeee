King Paimon is primarily associated with knowledge, arts, science, and influence, though he can bestow dignity and honor (which often leads to wealth).

1. Mammon (The Entity of Wealth & Prosperity)

If you are specifically looking for the "god of money" and luxury, you are referring to Mammon. In historical theology, Mammon is not just a demon but the personification of wealth and greed itself.

Mantra (Enn): "Tasa Mammon On Ca Lirach"

Pronunciation: Tah-sah Mam-on On Kah Lee-rahk.

Attributes: Financial gain, material abundance, greed, business success, and the accumulation of assets. He is often invoked to remove poverty or secure rapid financial growth.
