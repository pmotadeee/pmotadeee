The Philosophy of the Ritual

This ritual is designed for when you are beginning a major new phase of work—a new research paper, a business venture, or a creative endeavor.

Paimon is invoked first to bless the intellectual structure, the blueprint, and the influence the project will have.

Mammon is invoked second to bless the material reality, the funding, and the practical resources needed to make the idea real.

The Setup (The Altar of Industry)

Set up a clean workspace. You do not need a frightening "occult" setup. It should look like the desk of a powerful scholar-architect.

Symbols: Draw the sigils of Paimon and Mammon on separate pieces of paper. Place Paimon to the Northwest (if possible) and Mammon to the North.

Lighting: A yellow or gold candle for Paimon; a green or gold candle for Mammon.

The Focus: Crucially, place a physical representation of your project on the altar. This could be your notebook, a printed first draft of your research, or a USB drive containing your work.

Incense: Frankincense (to raise the vibration for Paimon) mixed with patchouli or cinnamon (to ground it for Mammon).

The Ritual Steps

1. The Center and Grounding Light the incense and candles. Stand before the altar. Take deep breaths. State your noble intent clearly to the universe.

"I stand here not for greed, but for growth. I seek knowledge to elevate humanity, and the resources to make that knowledge real. My work is the vessel."

2. The Invocation of the King (Paimon) Focus on Paimon’s sigil. Visualize a brilliant, intelligent yellow light illuminating your mind and your project notes. Chant his Enn with authority (vibrating the words deeply).

Chant: “Linan Tasa Jedan Paimon” (Repeat 3, 7, or 9 times until you feel a shift in the air—a clarity or "ringing" sensation).

Address: "Great King Paimon, Master of Arts and Sciences. I dedicate this intellectual labor to you. Grant me the insight to see what others miss. Grant this project the influence it needs to reach the right minds. Let it be a beacon of truth."

3. The Invocation of the Lord of Sustenance (Mammon) Focus on Mammon’s sigil. Visualize a heavy, rich, golden-green energy rising from the earth and filling the room, grounding the intellectual energy.

Chant: “Tasa Mammon On Ca Lirach” (Repeat 3, 7, or 9 times until the room feels heavier, denser, and charged with potential).

Address: "Lord Mammon, Engine of the World. I dedicate the physical manifestation of this work to you. Fuel this endeavor. Remove the barriers of scarcity. Let this project generate the abundance required to sustain itself and bring prosperity to all it touches."

4. The Consecration of the Work Place your hands on your research/project object on the altar.

"By the blueprint of Paimon and the fuel of Mammon, I consecrate this work. May it be intelligent. May it be prosperous. May it serve the greater good. It is done."

5. The Closing Sit in meditation with your project for a few minutes, visualizing its success. When you are finished, thank them.

"King Paimon, Lord Mammon, thank you for your presence and your patronage. Go in power. The rite is ended." Extinguish the candles. (Ideally, keep the sigils tucked inside your project notebook or under your computer as you work on it).
