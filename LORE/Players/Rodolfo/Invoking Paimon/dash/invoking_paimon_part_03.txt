History & Origins Mammon’s history is unique because he did not start as a pagan god who was later demonized (like Ba'al or Astarte). Instead, he began as a concept.

Etymology: The word comes from the Aramaic/Syriac word mamon, meaning "wealth," "profit," or "that in which one trusts."

Biblical Roots: In the New Testament (Matthew 6:24), the term was left untranslated ("You cannot serve both God and Mammon").

Personification: Over the centuries, medieval theologians (like Peter Binsfeld in 1589) personified this concept into a conscious entity, classifying Mammon as one of the Seven Princes of Hell, specifically representing the sin of Greed. In modern Demonolatry, he is viewed not just as "greed," but as the chaotic, raw force of Capitalism and Material Abundance.

Ritual & Devotional Practice Mammon is an "Earth" spirit—heavy, dense, and tangible. He does not deal in theories; he deals in results.

Direction: North (The direction of Earth and stability).

Element: Earth.

Colors: Gold (wealth) and Green (currency/growth).

Incense: Patchouli (earthy, associated with money), Frankincense, or Cinnamon (heating/speeding up results).

Offerings:

Direct Wealth: Coins (especially old or foreign currency), gold flakes, or placing your wallet on the altar.

Luxury: Fine whiskey, rich cigars, or high-quality chocolate. He appreciates the enjoyment of wealth, not just the hoarding of it.

The "Vibe": Rituals for Mammon are often business-like. You don't beg; you propose a transaction. "I will dedicate this project to you, and in return, you ensure its profitability."

2. King Paimon: The Master of Influence and Arts

History & Origins Paimon is a much older and more "formal" entity, appearing in the foundational grimoires of magic.

The Grimoires: He is the 9th Spirit of the Ars Goetia (the first book of the Lesser Key of Solomon). He is a King, described as being incredibly obedient to Lucifer.

Appearance: He is famously described as a man with a "glorious crown" riding a dromedary (camel), preceded by a host of spirits playing trumpets and cymbals. This signifies that Sound and Music are his domain.

Ancient Roots: Some scholars link him to Mesopotamian or djinn-like spirits due to the desert/camel imagery and his title of "King of the West."

Ritual & Devotional Practice Paimon is a Solar/Mercurial spirit—bright, loud, and intellectual. He is intense and expects high respect (dignity).

Direction: Northwest (This is his specific "House").

Element: Water (though often associated with Air due to his intellect and sound).

Colors: Yellow and Gold (Solar colors), sometimes White.

Incense: Frankincense (Solar) or Jasmine (Mental clarity).

Offerings:

Music: This is the best offering. Playing an instrument, singing, or even playing complex classical music during the ritual pleases him.

Art: Drawings, poetry, or creative works.

Sweets: He is known to enjoy sweet wines, honey, and fruits.

The "Vibe": Paimon can be overwhelming. He often arrives with a loud "ringing" sound in the ears. In rituals, you must remain calm and respectful but firm. He is excellent for manipulating how others perceive you (getting a promotion, winning a debate, becoming famous).

Key Synergy for You

Since you follow both, you have a powerful "duality" for success:

Paimon gives you the idea, the skill, and the social connection to open the door.

Mammon ensures that the door leads to hard cash and material assets.
